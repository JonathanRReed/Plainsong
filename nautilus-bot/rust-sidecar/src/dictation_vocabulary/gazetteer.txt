# Plainsong built-in vocabulary, version 1 (2026-10-24).
#
# Spellings speech recognizers commonly get wrong: developer tools and
# services, and given names and surnames from many languages whose spelling
# does not follow English pronunciation. Hand-curated for Plainsong from
# general knowledge (no third-party list was copied); covered by Plainsong's
# own licence. Product names are trademarks of their owners and appear only
# so they are spelled correctly.
#
# Plain English words are left out on purpose (Rust, Swift, Linear, Slack,
# Notion, Mark, Rose): a recognizer already spells them, and an ordinary word
# is never replaced.
#
# Format: one term per line, `Term | spoken form, spoken form`. A spoken form
# is a plain-English respelling of how the term is usually said, used only to
# match the audio. Lines starting with `#` and blank lines are ignored.
